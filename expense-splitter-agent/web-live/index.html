💡 The cent that has to go somewhere

100.00 split three ways is 3333.333… cents each. Rounding each share independently loses a cent; SplitKit uses the largest-remainder method so the total is preserved exactly. 


    

  


  
  

    

      

        
🤝 Settle up in the fewest transfers

        
Balances reduced to the minimum number of payments that clears the whole book.


        

        

        

      


      

        
🔢 Why it is a minimum

        
Two independent lower bounds, both checked against the plan.

        

        

          A payment can clear at most one person’s whole balance, so you always need at least
          max(number of creditors, number of debtors) transfers. When the plan matches that bound it is
          provably minimal — and SplitKit says so instead of just claiming it.
